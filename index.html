FAQ

Questions Answered

Everything you need to know before starting your project with us.

Still have questions? Contact us →
What areas do you serve?+

We are based in Sokoto State with two locations — our head office at No. 11 Ahmadu Bello Way and our branch along Pinnacle Hotel Road, Tamaje. We serve clients across all of Sokoto State and take on projects throughout Northwest Nigeria.

How do I get a quote for my project?+

Simply fill out our contact form below or call us directly at 08038466792 / 07036252792. We offer free consultations and will provide a detailed, transparent quote within 24–48 hours of your inquiry.

Can you handle a project from design through to completion?+

Yes. We offer end-to-end concept design and planning, construction and execution, civil engineering and infrastructure work, plus fabrication and installation — so one team can carry your project from first sketch to finished building.

How long does a typical project take?+

Project timelines vary based on scope. Small fabrication jobs typically take 3–7 days. Larger construction and civil engineering projects can take several weeks to months. We commit to every agreed timeline and have a strong track record of on-time delivery.

Do you provide a warranty on your work?+

Yes. All our construction and fabrication work comes with a warranty. Structural and installation work carries a 2-year workmanship warranty, and roofing systems are backed by the manufacturer's warranty plus our installation guarantee.

What happens after my building is completed?+

Our relationship doesn't end at handover. Through our post-completion facility management service, we provide ongoing maintenance, repairs, and facility oversight to protect your investment for years to come.
